# Privacy requests

People whose data we hold (players who signed in with Discord) can ask for a copy of it, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. The service is based in the UK and its players are mostly in the UK, so the UK GDPR applies: **answer within one month**, free of charge, and only after being reasonably sure who is asking.

## What players can do by themselves

- **A copy:** *Download my data* in the companion app saves a JSON file with everything held about them (`POST /v1/export`; what it holds and leaves out is described on the privacy page and in `docs/PRIVACY.md`).
- **Deletion:** *Delete my data* in the companion app erases it from the live service at once. Recovery copies age out: Cloudflare's 7-day history and our own daily backups (14 days). See `docs/runbooks/BACKUPS.md`.
- **Visibility:** public or private, chosen when signing in.

## When someone writes to us

1. **Check who they are.** The easy proof is the Discord account: ask them to reply from, or send a message through, the Discord account they signed in with, or to tell you their player id (it is on their public page, `/ladder`) and a character name only they would know. Do not hand over data to an unverified request.
2. **A copy:** tell them to use *Download my data* (it proves they hold the key). If they cannot, you can build the same file from the database by hand for the verified player; use `server/src/sqlStore.ts`'s `exportPlayer` as the list of what to include.
3. **Correction:** most of the data is generated by the game and the add-on (cards, events) and cannot be edited, because the ledger is hash-chained. Names (a Discord display name, character names) come from Discord and the game. If something is wrong, explain where it comes from; the player can start a new binder or delete their data.
4. **Deletion:** point them to *Delete my data*. If they cannot use the app, delete the player by hand once verified (`deletePlayer` in `server/src/sqlStore.ts`: it removes the player and archived binders), and say that recovery copies age out in at most 14 days. **If a restore ever brings a deleted account back, delete it again before reopening the service.**
5. **Banned accounts** are kept so that a ban cannot be dodged (this is on the privacy page).
6. **Reports about someone:** a report another player made about a person is not given to that person, and the reporter's identity is not shared.
7. Reply in writing, keep the request and the reply for a year, and note anything you could not do and why.

## The contact address

The privacy page shows a contact address once one is set. Until then it points to the app's buttons and the GitHub issue tracker (with a warning not to put personal details in a public issue).

To set one up (free, a few minutes, in the Cloudflare dashboard for `worldofwarcards.co.uk`):

1. **Email, Email Routing, Get started.** Cloudflare adds the DNS records it needs.
2. **Create an address**, for example `privacy@worldofwarcards.co.uk`, and make it **forward to** your own inbox (Cloudflare asks you to confirm that inbox once).
3. Send a test message to it from another account.
4. Set the address on the server: in `server/wrangler.toml` add `PRIVACY_EMAIL = "privacy@worldofwarcards.co.uk"` to the `vars` of both environments, deploy staging and production. The privacy page then shows it as a link.

Email Routing only forwards incoming mail. To reply *from* that address (so people see it), use your own mail's "send as" feature, or reply from your own address and say so.

## Other things the law expects (a short list, not legal advice)

- **Registering with the ICO.** In the UK, organisations that handle personal data usually pay a small data protection fee unless exempt (some small not-for-profits are). Check the ICO's self-assessment at ico.org.uk when the service has real players, and keep the answer here.
- **A record of what you hold and why** is the privacy page plus `docs/PRIVACY.md`; keep them true when the data changes.
- **A breach** (someone getting at players' data) must be reported to the ICO within 72 hours if it puts people at risk, and players told if it is likely to harm them. The first steps are in `docs/runbooks/ALERTS.md` and `docs/runbooks/BACKUPS.md`; replace secrets (`TAPE_SECRET` aside, see `BACKUPS.md`) and sign everyone out by revoking keys.
