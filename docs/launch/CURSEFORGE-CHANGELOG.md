Collect cards as you explore World of Warcraft: Forever, fill your binder and trade with other collectors.

- Browse zone and dungeon collections, search by creature or set, and filter owned or missing cards.
- Collect sealed zone packs in a separate satchel. Common, Rare and Epic packs reveal one, three or five cards.
- Trade cards and packs, or send a gift. Both players review and accept the offer.
- Find alternate collectible editions, earn achievements and equip binder titles.
- Collect faction bonus cards and expanded dungeon/raid lists. Known unobtainable targets are excluded from completion goals; existing copies are kept.
- Join timed seasonal collections when a season is running, alongside your normal Ranked binder.
- Use the separate Windows companion for Ranked play, trading, gifting, seasons and the online ladder. Casual collecting works with the addon alone.

Recent improvements include the option to open at your current zone, Owned/Unowned index filters, in-game bug-report links, guild announcements, trade-window fixes and more reliable Ranked setup with the companion. Sounds and 3D portraits can be switched off in Settings.

**Existing players:** install companion 1.0.2 before addon 1.0.14 and keep your game's saved data. Older finds, packs and completed pages retain their original rules. Updating does not require starting a new binder or losing your collection.

**Known issue:** some DirectX 12 sessions can get stuck loading while repeatedly minimising and restoring. Warcards' 3D models may make this worse; reduced model preloading is not a confirmed fix. If chat responds, press Enter, type `/reload` and press Enter again. Otherwise close the game with Alt+F4 and relaunch; unsaved finds may be lost. See [known issues](https://github.com/Imokim2/azeroth-card-binder/blob/main/KNOWN-ISSUES.md).
