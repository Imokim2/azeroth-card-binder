## 0.7.0 — 2026-11-13 (companion 0.5.0, server 0.5.0)

- **Group-test candidate.** Add-on 0.7.0, companion 0.5.0 (ledger protocol 9) and server 0.5.0 ship together: seasons, the second set of drop rates and the launch collection below. Nothing resets: cards, packs, dust, titles and history are kept, and every earlier Ranked history replays as before. Install the companion first. A companion older than 0.5.0 cannot upload a binder that has recorded the new markers (`client_too_old`: update it); an older add-on keeps working on the old rates until it is updated. Release steps and checks: `docs/launch/RELEASE-0.7.0.md`.

- **Half the cards are held back for seasons (ledger spec 9, `mode/launch1`).** 1,712 of the 3,423 cards (ordinary creatures; every elite, boss, critter and gathering card stays) are marked `seasonal` from a generated list (`AzerothCardBinder/LaunchCollection.lua`, `tools/launch-collection.js`). Until a season that features one has started, such a card does not drop, is not in packs, cannot be crafted and is not shown in a binder that does not own it; from the season's start it is available to everyone, for good. A page is finished by its launch cards alone (the catalog's sets name them in `required`), so every page is finishable and released cards are extras. Nothing is deleted or taken away: owned copies stay and show, and every earlier history replays exactly as it did, because the rule starts at a once-only marker (`launch1`, after `chase2`). The server enforces it (`retired`), taking the earlier of the event's time and its own, and supplies the release list to the add-on through the companion (`sync.seasonal.released`). Add-on, companion (spec 9) and server ship together. See `docs/proposals/SMALLER-LAUNCH-COLLECTION.md`.
- **A second set of drop rates (ledger spec 8, `mode/chase2`).** Critters are four times as likely per attempt (a critter that needed about 1,800 attempts now needs about 450), other ordinary cards 20% more likely, and chase cards on bosses, rare spawns, gathering and epics are unchanged. Old histories are not touched: the catalog keeps both sets of rates (`dropProfile.rate` and `rate2`), the add-on writes `chase2` once before its first roll after upgrading (after `chase1`, and after any run of misses at the old rates), and the server replays each event at the rates in force when it was rolled. The luck checks start afresh at `chase2`. The companion now says spec 8; the server refuses a binder on the new rates from an older companion (`client_too_old`). Add-on, companion and server must ship together (`docs/runbooks/DROP-BALANCE.md`).
- Added seasonal collections alongside Ranked: a shared manual or seeded card list, fixed dates, fresh-acquisition progress, completion rewards for everyone and first-finisher bonuses. The Seasons tab uses the existing binder pages, previews and title system; earned packs go to the normal satchel.
- Added server-owned, single-use pack reward grants and protocol-7 validation. Old inventory, Casual gains and crafting do not count toward seasons. Verified gains retain their ordinary binder copies. Seasonal sync follows saved-file changes without idle polling, and recovery preserves reward claims and titles.
- Added an inactive season template, local preview/scheduling tool and deployment/group-test guide in `docs/SEASONS.md`. No live season is created or published by these changes.
- Seasons replaces Trade in the binder's top row. New trades and gifts begin from **Trade cards or packs** in a player's right-click menu. Removed initiation shortcuts from the inspected binder, Wanted, satchel and slash commands; active offer selection and incoming invitation acceptance remain.

## 0.6.4 — 2026-11-12 (companion 0.4.4)

- New binders default to Ranked after a verified companion setup. The local setup pairing prevents an unrelated or stale sync file from choosing a mode. Without it, a one-time choice appears before the first card or pack is rolled. Casual requires an explicit choice, the mode stays visible in the binder, and switching away from Ranked asks for confirmation. Existing Casual cards are kept and are not silently relabelled as Ranked.
- The companion brings a new history conflict into view and offers Repair sync. It rebuilds routine sync data first and keeps five recent synced saves per account without extra network requests. A backup restore requires a closed game, explicit approval, fresh server/local checks and preservation of all current progress. Both original files are kept, current preferences survive, and no reset or deletion is used as a repair. Ambiguous cases stay unchanged.
- The trade window and pack picker use the binder leather, parchment, brass and ink palette. Offer and history panels match the binder pages.

### Earlier server and sync changes (included in 0.6.0)

- **A fuller admin panel, with its own sign-in and a record of what is done.** `/admin` now has three tabs: **Flags** as before (with a link to the player's page), **Players** (search by name, character or player id; a page for each player with their characters, score, binder mode, uploads, trades, flags and report count, never a Discord id or a key; **Ban...** with a reason, **Unban**, and **Delete their data...** which asks for the player id to be typed) and **Activity**, a record of every flag decision, ban, unban, deletion and rebuild with who did it (`GET /v1/admin/audit`, a new `admin_audit` table: migration 0012, which holds no names). **Cloudflare Access** can now sit in front of the panel (`ACCESS_TEAM_DOMAIN` and `ACCESS_AUD`): the Worker checks Access's signed token itself, so only people on the Access list, on the real address, get as far as the admin token, and the record names them by email. Without those settings the token alone works as before. Setup, lock-out recovery and what the panel cannot do are in `docs/runbooks/ADMIN.md`.
- **Reset and restore a player's binder from the admin panel.** **Reset their binder...** puts a binder aside in a private archive (nothing is deleted), takes the player off the ladder and moves them to a new roll-tape epoch; because their add-on still holds the old history, the server also refuses an upload that starts from before the reset (new error `binder_reset`, migration 0013) until they start a new binder. **Restore this binder...** brings an archived binder back with its chain and epoch, putting the current one aside in its place, so either can be undone. Both are recorded in the activity record. Players found by name include those whose binder was put aside. The companion app shows the new error as its code ("The server refused it (binder_reset)"); a friendlier message needs a companion release.
- Companion syncing now follows game saves instead of a ten-minute polling timer. The leaderboard stays cached between saved-progress syncs, required work retries with backoff, and saves received during an upload are queued. Update checks are kept for six hours during normal use.
- Wanted lists stay in game and are shared directly between players. The companion no longer sends or fetches them; the web board is retired, with cheap empty responses for older companions.

- **Lighter on the server.** Public answers (the ladder, firsts, the wanted board, public player pages, site pages, the release announcement) are now shared at Cloudflare's edge for a minute or five instead of being worked out for every request; the companion app asks for standings every 30 minutes instead of 5, and for Ranked roll tape only when the add-on is running low instead of on every pass. A deleted or newly private player can still appear on a public page for up to a minute. Estimates and costs: `docs/runbooks/CAPACITY.md`.
- **Download my data.** The companion window has a **Download my data…** button that saves a copy of everything the server holds about you as a JSON file (`POST /v1/export`, signed with your key): your account, keys, characters, every event of every binder you have had, saved state, score, trades, finished sets, wanted cards and the reports you sent. Reports others made about you, reviewers' notes and server secrets are left out. The privacy page says so, has a "Questions about your data" section and shows a contact address once `PRIVACY_EMAIL` is set (`docs/runbooks/PRIVACY-REQUESTS.md`).
- **Capacity measured.** `npm run bench` in `server/` times the heaviest requests: tape requests take 20 to 55 ms and a full Ranked upload about 60 ms, well over the free Workers plan's 10 ms of CPU. `docs/runbooks/CAPACITY.md` recommends the paid plan before launch.
- **Code signing is shelved.** `docs/CODE-SIGNING.md` records what SignPath Foundation's free programme for open-source projects needs and how the project measures up, and the release workflow has steps that would sign the installer through it once the repository variable `SIGNPATH_ORGANIZATION_ID` is set. We are not applying for now: the installer stays unsigned, and downloads carry SHA-256 checksums.
- **A review page for the people who run the service.** `/admin` lists the open flags (players the checks held back), the reports about each player, and buttons to clear, confirm, ban and unban, over the existing admin routes. The admin token is typed in and kept in the page's memory only; the page runs one script that the content security policy pins by hash, and shows everything from the server as text.
- **Public wording.** The help and FAQ pages describe the public release (real downloads, the unsigned-installer warning, GitHub for reports), and bug-report and idea templates ask for the details that help (`/acb version`, the companion version, `/acb log`).
- **`node tools/smoke.js`** checks a running server after a deploy: health, the signed release and its downloads, every public page and its safety headers, and that the admin doors are shut (`--deep` also checks the files' SHA-256).

## 0.5.0 - 2026-11-12

- **First public release.** Add-on 0.5.0 with companion app 0.3.0 (the companion's own version is separate from the add-on's).
- **Public downloads.** The website's download page now shows the addon ZIP and the companion installer, with sizes and SHA-256 checksums, as soon as a signed release names them, and links to the project's GitHub releases page once `RELEASES_URL` is set. Pushing a `v*` tag builds both files and publishes a GitHub release (`.github/workflows/release.yml`). The installer file is now named `World-of-Warcards-Setup-<version>.exe`. The project is licensed GPL-3.0-or-later (`LICENSE`), with a public README and `SECURITY.md`.
- **Backups.** `tools/backup-db.js` takes a checked copy of the database outside Cloudflare, keeps the newest 14 and refuses a folder inside the repository; `docs/runbooks/BACKUPS.md` explains Cloudflare's point-in-time restore, the daily schedule and how to restore. The privacy page now says that deleted data leaves backup copies as they age out (7 and 14 days).
- **A player who went Ranked on the old add-on and then updated can sync again.** The old Ranked switch written before the update is accepted when another mode switch follows it directly (nothing was rolled under it); a roll after it, or it alone, is still refused.
- **Update alerts.** The server now says which versions are current (`GET /v1/releases`: the newest companion app and add-on, and the oldest add-on it accepts). The companion app checks every half hour, reads the add-on version from its `.toc` file and from the saved data the game wrote, and says in its window when the add-on or the app is out of date, when the add-on is too old, or when a newer add-on is installed but the game has not loaded it yet (use /reload). It also passes the newest and oldest add-on version on in `Data/Sync.lua`, so the add-on can say it too: a line in chat a few seconds after you log in, and `/acb version` at any time. The add-on writes the version it runs as into its saved data (`addonVersion`).
- **Updating from the companion window.** When a release has been signed with our release key, the window offers **Update add-on** and **Update companion**. The add-on zip is downloaded, checked against the signed size and SHA-256, unpacked and checked again before it replaces anything; the old copy is kept in the app's data folder, `Data/Sync.lua` is never replaced, and a failed install puts the old files back. The companion asks first, then downloads its installer, checks it the same way and runs it. Anything not signed with the key built into the app is refused. Publishing a release: `docs/runbooks/RELEASES.md` and `tools/release-manifest.js`.
- **Ranked pauses for an add-on the server no longer accepts.** The app tells the server which add-on version the player last ran; below the oldest accepted version (`MIN_ADDON`) the server gives no new roll tape (`addon_too_old`), the add-on stops Ranked rolls once it learns the minimum (or when its tape runs out) and says why, and the companion window says to update. Casual is never affected and tape already handed out stays valid, so nothing earned is lost.
- **Another player's binder is the real binder, and you can trade from it.** Viewing a player opens the same pages as your own binder, filled with their cards and read-only, with the sets index, a **Wanted** tab listing what they are looking for (and whether you own each card) and a line saying how many spares each of you could swap. Press **Trade** on one of their wanted cards that you own: you give that card, their binder opens for you to pick the card you want in return, and a button on that card's panel sends the offer. They see it in chat and on the Trade tab, where **Accept offer** or **Decline** answers it (or `/acb trade accept` and `/acb trade decline`); accepting sends the card you asked for and confirms the pair, and the swap is made on both sides. The older Offer, Listen and Reply steps on the Trade tab still work.
- **Viewing binders and trading now work on Forever.** Forever has no realms: a character has two names ("Filfred Whizzlebang"), and the game addresses a whisper to that, with a space. The add-on sent "filfred-whizzlebang", which the game accepted and delivered to nobody, so binder views and trades got no answer. Whispers now go out as "First Second", and `/acb view` takes a two-word name. `/acb whisperform hyphen` goes back to the old form if some server needs it. With `/acb diag` on, `/acb log` now shows every addon whisper sent and received, with the game's result.
- **Card packs.** Any enemy you defeat has a 1 in 500 chance to drop a pack, whether or not its own card drops. A pack holds five cards from the zone or dungeon it dropped in: common cards are common, epic ones rarer, legendary ones rarest (the odds are 70, 20, 8 and 2 out of 100 for common, rare, epic and legendary, and a zone with none of a rarity shares that share among the rest; creature zones have no Rare cards, so there it goes to the others). Open them from the **Packs** button on the binder window (it shows how many you hold) or with `/acb packs`: the pack shakes and bursts, five face-down cards are dealt, and you click each one to flip it over. The cards are in your binder as soon as the pack is opened, and closing the window early tells chat what you did not turn over. **Ranked packs are checked by the server**: finding a pack and each of its cards come from the roll tape, so a Ranked pack needs five tape entries (reload so the companion app can bring more if you have fewer). Packs opened in Casual count as Casual drops, like any Casual card. Ledger specification version 3; the server takes versions 2 and 3.
- **Cards from kills you can't loot.** Critters (rabbits, rats, squirrels and the like) and any creature with nothing to loot never opened a loot window, so their cards could not drop. Now a creature you have targeted alive, that dies while you are in combat and was not tapped by someone else, gets its one roll about two and a half seconds after it dies, unless looting it rolled it first. A creature is still only rolled once, so looting it too gives nothing extra. Bosses, gathering nodes and corpses you did not see die are not affected. A switch in Settings ("Cards from kills you can't loot", on by default) turns it off; the diagnostics log (`/acb log`) shows each kill it saw or skipped. Ranked works as before: the kill uses the next tape entry.
- **Report a player.** `/acb report <player or target> [cheating, trading or other] [a short note]`, or the **Report** button in a player's binder window (click twice). The report waits in the saved file; the companion app sends it the next time it syncs, and the server keeps it for the people who run the service to read. Three different players reporting the same player within a week opens one review flag; nothing is hidden or removed automatically, so a few accounts cannot push someone off the ladder. Reports are limited to one a day about the same player and twenty a day in all, are covered in the privacy page, and are erased when you delete your data. Server: `POST /v1/report` and, for reviewers, `GET /v1/admin/reports`.
- **Ranked now checks every attempt, not just totals.** Ledger specification version 2. In Ranked the add-on writes every attempt into the ledger, as a drop or as a short run of misses on one card (a new `miss` event), and the server replays each one against its own roll tape. A drop cannot be invented, and one cannot be left out by recording it as a miss, because the entry decides. Entering Ranked writes a `ranked2` mode event and starts every card's stack of misses from zero. The old totals check is gone. The roll tape is now handed out in batches of 1,000 (topped up when 250 or fewer remain) instead of 10,000, so a player can read far less of it ahead of time; heavy players may need to `/reload` about once an hour to let the companion app top it up. Uploads in version 1 are no longer accepted: update the companion app and the add-on together. Binders already in Ranked switch over automatically the next time the add-on loads. The ledger grows (up to one event per attempt on a different card), so uploads are bigger.
- **Wanted tab.** The cards players are looking for, in one place: the tab lists your own wanted cards (click to remove, or add one by name) and what other players are looking for, with the players who want a card you have spare at the top and marked. Click a name to offer a trade. The list comes from the public board, which the companion app uploads and brings back (public players only; so it is as fresh as the last sync and your last reload), and from **Check my target**, which asks the player you target and shows their answer at once (they need the add-on and to allow viewing). The binder view of another player now shows what they are looking for too. Server: `POST /v1/wanted`, `GET /v1/wanted` and a `/wanted` page.
- **Trading between players:** the binder view of another player now lists the spares they have that you need and the spares you have that they need (cards on your wanted list first), with an **Offer a trade** button. `/acb ask <player> <card>` (or `/acb ask target <card>`, or **Ask target** on a missing card's page) tells a player you are looking for a card; they see it only if they own it, with a link and how to start a swap. **Trading distance:** like the game's own trading, a trade now needs your partner targeted and within about 11 yards (a **My target** button on the Trade tab fills the name in); switch it off in Settings. The distance is a nicety of play, not a safeguard: the server checks every trade regardless.
- Server: when a trade half is never matched and the partner has uploaded since, the partner is flagged (`trade-half-missing`) instead of the player they took the card from.

- **Website pages**, served by the server: the ladder and first-to-finish list, a player's page with a read-only view of their binder, fair play and privacy. Plain, safe HTML with no scripts or game art.
- **Windows installer** for the companion app (`npm run dist` in `app/`). Unsigned, so Windows will show an "unknown publisher" warning.
- Cards that came from Casual play are now followed through any number of trades, not just the first.

- Server hardening: per-address rate limits (a general one and a strict one for registration, reset, deletion and admin routes), and a data-deletion route with a **Delete my data…** button in the companion app. Deleting erases the player's record and archived binders; a banned player's record is kept.

- **Start a new binder.** `/acb newbinder` (then `/acb newbinder confirm`) erases the binder on that account, keeps a copy of the old collection in the saved file and starts an empty one. The companion app notices that the saved data is not the binder the server holds and offers to start a new binder on the server, which archives the old record and gives the new one a fresh roll tape. Needed to join the Ranked ladder from a binder that already has Casual drops. Once a day at most.

- **Only Ranked counts for the ladder.** A drop rolled in Casual mode cannot be checked by the server, so a player with any Casual drop is no longer listed on the ladder or in the firsts feed, and cards traded in from such a player stop counting for the receiver. The Ladder tab says plainly why a binder cannot join, and `/acb mode casual` warns before you leave Ranked. To join the ladder, switch to Ranked before your first drop.

- Companion app window: an Electron window and tray icon with the three steps (sign in with Discord, choose the game folder, watch the sync). The key is kept encrypted by Windows. Run it with `npm start` in `app/`; there is no installer yet.

- Discord sign-in for the companion app and server: the player signs in with Discord in their own browser; the app never sees a Discord password or secret, and the server keeps only the Discord id. Registration opens once the Discord application's secret is set on the server.

- Companion app core: the sync engine joins everything into one loop (upload, roll tape, standings, `Data/Sync.lua`) that runs whenever the game finishes writing its saved file. Still no window around it.
- Server: a flag review API (clear, confirm, ban) behind an admin token, luck that a reviewer has cleared is not flagged again, and a rebuild job that recomputes everything derived from the stored batches (a page of players at a time, with a dry run).

- **Ladder tab** in the binder: the top of the Ranked ladder, where you stand, and who finished each set first, with a "(verified)" mark beside ranked players in the trade window and the binder view. The standings come from the companion app, so they need the app and a reload; without it the tab says so.
- Server: trade matching (the two halves of a trade are paired; a half nobody confirms within a week is flagged and stops counting), the firsts feed (`GET /v1/firsts`) and the ranked players' characters on the leaderboard.

- **The zone card bar is now green and takes the reputation bar's place** (the bar above the experience bar), in the same green, instead of the honour bar. It hands the reputation bar back when switched off.
- **Trading works with any card**, not just duplicates, including a last copy. Trade panel text says when an offer is your only copy. The shared rules and test vectors allow a trade out of a last copy.
- **Viewing other players' binders** now explains itself: you are told when the request was sent, when someone keeps their binder private, when nothing comes back, and when only part arrives. Binder parts go out at a pace the game accepts and a refused part is sent again. Before, a binder of more than about ten messages could be cut short without any sign.

- **Zone card bar.** A thin bar takes the honour bar's place above the experience bar (or sits above the experience bar if there is no honour bar) and shows how many cards you own in the zone, dungeon, raid or battleground you are in, and changes by itself as you travel. Hover for the detail and click to open the binder; Shift-drag moves it. Turn it off in Settings or with `/acb bar` and the honour bar returns; `/acb bar reset` clears a dragged position; `/acb bar debug` says which bars it found.

- **Stacking drop chance.** Every miss now raises a card's chance for the next attempt by its listed rate (a 1% card goes 1%, 2%, 3% and so on, up to certain), and a drop starts it over. This replaces the old bad-luck guarantee and makes cards drop far more often: a 1% card now takes about 13 kills on average instead of about 63. The step is one setting (`ACB.stackFactor` in `Catalog.lua`) if it proves too generous.
- Card names are never cut off with "..." any more: the text shrinks to fit the card, the binder grid and the card page, and breaks onto two lines only for the very longest names.
- Zephras Isle, the Skyborne starting zone, is in the binder with its eight rare creatures. Its ordinary creatures follow when their ids are published.

- The binder keeps a tamper-evident ledger of every card found, crafted, recycled and traded, and checks it each time you log in. If a card count is edited by hand, the Settings tab and `/acb verify` say "Binder unverified". Nothing about playing changes. Saved data moves to version 2 on first load; cards, dust, titles and trade history are kept.
- Ranked mode (needs the companion app): `/acb mode ranked` or the Settings button. Ranked rolls use random numbers the server issues, so every drop can be rechecked. If the supply runs out, Ranked rolling pauses with a message until the app refills it; `/acb mode casual` returns to normal play. Binders that hold cards from before the ledger cannot enter Ranked yet.
- Every roll is now counted, kept apart from the bad-luck counter, and the add-on remembers recently rolled corpses across a reload so the same corpse cannot be rolled twice.

- Engineering foundations (Phase 0 of the [roadmap](docs/ROADMAP.md)): repository layout, continuous integration, the written ledger specification with shared test vectors, contributing guide, decision records, and a release script. Nothing changes for players.

## 0.4.0 - 2026-11-12

- **Distinct boss variants.** All sixteen dungeon editions now use deliberately different actions, viewpoints and framing from Signature: crouches, prayer, salutes, roars, wide weapon strikes and contrasting spell casts. Animated profiles have individual repeat intervals and supported alternative actions. The studio describes each composition; ordinary card poses and variant drop rates are unchanged. Foe Reaper's studio previews also have new close/wide compositions.
- **Simpler trades and gifts.** Choose a player, use the searchable card picker and click Start trade. The other player's window opens automatically; both players check You give / You receive and click Accept. Gift a card leaves the recipient's side empty. Offer changes clear acceptance; cancellations and delayed messages cannot approve a different revision or apply the same transfer twice. Both players need addon 0.4.0 or newer for this flow. Gifts use the existing server-checked transfer history and count towards trade milestones.
- **Consistent controls and clean tabs.** Addon action and close buttons use the binder's brown-and-brass style. Opening Wanted hides every previous page, and the section label no longer overlaps the Settings tab.
- **Portrait loading.** Opening a collection's index also warms its portraits. Slow models get a bounded retry; visible cards recover requests made while hidden. Pack faces request their models and backgrounds during opening, before flipping, and check again on reveal. Temporary artwork fallbacks no longer prevent later loading.
- **Zone card bar recovery.** Hidden, collapsed and off-screen tracking bars are ignored when choosing its position. The bar follows later layout changes, saved positions stay on-screen, and `/acb bar reset` turns it on as well as resetting its position.
